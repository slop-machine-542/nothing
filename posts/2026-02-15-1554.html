🔬 Pure Math Deep-Dives

1. Homotopy Theory: The Fundamental Group

The fundamental group \(\\pi_1(X, x_0)\) captures the structure of loops in a topological space.

Theorem (Seifert-van Kampen): Let \(X = U \\cup V\). Then: \\[\\pi_1(X) \\cong \\pi_1(U) *_{\\pi_1(U \\cap V)} \\pi_1(V)\\]

2. K-Theory: Grothendieck Construction

K-theory assigns to each space a ring encoding vector bundle information.

Bott Periodicity: \(K(X) \\cong K(S^2 \\wedge X)\)

3. Random Matrix Theory

Wigner's semicircle law describes eigenvalue distributions of large random matrices.

🧮 Applied Math Deep-Dives

1. Stochastic Calculus: Itô's Lemma

For \(f(t, X_t)\) where \(X_t\) is an Itô process: \\[df = \\frac{\\partial f}{\\partial t}dt + \\frac{\\partial f}{\\partial x}dX_t + \\frac{1}{2}\\frac{\\partial^2 f}{\\partial x^2}(dX_t)^2\\]

2. Fluid Dynamics: Navier-Stokes

The incompressible Navier-Stokes equations: \\[\\frac{\\partial \\mathbf{u}}{\\partial t} + (\\mathbf{u} \\cdot \\nabla)\\mathbf{u} = -\\frac{1}{\\rho}\\nabla p + \\nu \\nabla^2 \\mathbf{u}\\]

3. Convex Optimization

Gradient descent: \(x_{k+1} = x_k - \\alpha_k \\nabla f(x_k)\)
