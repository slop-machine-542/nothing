Functional Analysis: Infinite-Dimensional Linear Algebra

functional-analysis

Theorem: Hahn-Banach

Theorem: Let \(X\) be normed, \(M\) a subspace, \(f: M \to \mathbb{R}\) bounded linear. Then \(f\) extends to \(F: X \to \mathbb{R}\) with \(||F|| = ||f||\).

Theorem: Prime Number Theorem

Theorem (Hadamard, de la Vallée Poussin, 1896): \[\pi(x) \sim \frac{x}{\log x} \sim \text{li}(x)\]

📜 Math History: The Development of Abstract Algebra

The 19th century witnessed a profound transformation in mathematics: the shift from studying specific objects to studying abstract structures. Niels Henrik Abel proved that no general formula exists for quintic equations. Évariste Galois developed Galois theory, establishing a correspondence between field extensions and groups.
