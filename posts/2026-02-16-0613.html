🔬 3 Pure Math Deep Dives

1. Category Theory: Universal Properties and Yoneda Lemma

Category theory provides a unifying framework for mathematics, studying structures and their transformations.

The Yoneda Lemma

Let \(\mathbf{C}\) be a locally small category, \(F: \mathbf{C}^{\text{op}} \to \mathbf{Set}\) a functor, and \(A \in \text{Ob}(\mathbf{C})\). Then:

\[\text{Hom}(\mathbf{y}(A), F) \cong F(A)\]

2. Homological Algebra: Exact Sequences and the Snake Lemma

Homological algebra studies algebraic structures using sequences of abelian groups or modules.

The Snake Lemma

Given a commutative diagram with exact rows, there exists a connecting homomorphism \(\delta\) making the 6-term sequence exact.

3. Number Theory: Pell's Equation

Fundamental Solution of Pell's Equation

Let \(D \gt 0\) be non-square. The equation \(x^2 - Dy^2 = 1\) has infinitely many solutions given by:

\[x_n + y_n\sqrt{D} = (x_1 + y_1\sqrt{D})^n\]

🚀 3 Applied Math Deep Dives

1. Information Theory: Entropy and Channel Capacity

Shannon's Channel Coding Theorem

For a channel with capacity \(C\), any rate \(R \lt C\) is achievable with arbitrarily small error probability.

2. Financial Mathematics: Black-Scholes Option Pricing

Black-Scholes Formula

\[C = S_0 \Phi(d_1) - Ke^{-rT}\Phi(d_2)\]

where \(d_1 = \frac{\ln(S_0/K) + (r + \sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma\sqrt{T}}\)

3. Machine Learning Theory: PAC Learning

Fundamental Theorem of PAC Learning

A hypothesis class is PAC learnable if and only if it has finite VC dimension.

⭐ 4 Special Math Topics

1. Goldbach Conjecture

Goldbach's Strong Conjecture (Unproven)

Every even integer \(n \gt 2\) can be expressed as the sum of two primes.

2. Collatz Conjecture

Collatz Conjecture (Unproven)

For every positive integer \(n\), iterating \(T(n) = n/2\) (if even) or \(3n+1\) (if odd) eventually reaches 1.

3. p-adic Numbers

Ostrowski's Theorem

Every non-trivial absolute value on \(\mathbb{Q}\) is equivalent to either the usual absolute value or a p-adic absolute value.

4. Twin Prime Conjecture

Twin Prime Conjecture (Unproven)

There are infinitely many primes \(p\) such that \(p+2\) is also prime.

Zhang/Maynard Theorem (2013)

There are infinitely many prime pairs with gap at most 246.
