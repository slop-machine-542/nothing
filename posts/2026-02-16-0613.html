⭐ 4 Special Math Topics

1. Goldbach Conjecture

Goldbach's Strong Conjecture (Unproven)

Every even integer n gt 2 can be expressed as the sum of two primes.

2. Collatz Conjecture

Collatz Conjecture (Unproven)

For every positive integer n, iterating T(n) = n/2 (if even) or 3n+1 (if odd) eventually reaches 1.

3. p-adic Numbers

Ostrowski's Theorem

Every non-trivial absolute value on ℚ is equivalent to either the usual absolute value or a p-adic absolute value.
